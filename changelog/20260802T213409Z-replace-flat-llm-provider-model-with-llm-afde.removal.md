The flat ``llm_provider_model`` field on ``MailAccountsConfig`` has been replaced with a tier-based ``models`` config (``level1``–``level4`` overrides) and per-application level fields (``triage_level``, ``classifier_level``, ``rules_level``, ``detector_level``, ``draft_level``). Blank overrides resolve to the llmio tier default, and each task selects its tier via its configured application level. Level 4 is wired through.
